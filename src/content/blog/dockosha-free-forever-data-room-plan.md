## What this free plan does well

DocKosha Free is strongest when you need a controlled sharing workflow:

DocKosha Free is not unlimited, and it is not positioned as a full enterprise VDR replacement for every use case.

It is a practical starting point. Upgrade when limits on storage, bandwidth, teammates, branding, domains, or file-type support become blockers.
