# Free Secure Document Sharing vs Free Virtual Data Room

Use free secure document sharing when one protected file solves the job. Use a free virtual data room when external review becomes a multi-document process.

That is the practical boundary most teams care about.

## Best for secure document sharing (single-document mode)

- one sensitive report, deck, contract, or board packet
- short-lived review where folder structure is unnecessary
- sender needs control over access/download/watermark behavior

## Best for virtual data rooms (multi-document mode)

- diligence workflows with many related files
- investor, buyer, vendor, or client review processes
- cases where consistent permissions and room context matter
- workflows where engagement across documents drives follow-up

## Not for (on free plan)

- very large or high-traffic external distribution
- teams that require many collaborators immediately
- workflows needing non-PDF uploads before upgrade
- heavy white-label/domain requirements from the first day

## Honest claim boundaries

Free secure sharing and free VDR tools are about controlled starts, not full enterprise coverage.

DocKosha Free is designed for lightweight workflows and evaluation. It should not be sold as a replacement for every enterprise VDR requirement. The right time to upgrade is when scale, collaboration, branding, domain, or file-type needs outgrow free limits.

## FAQs

**Is a virtual data room just secure file sharing?**  
No. Secure sharing is one part of a VDR; a VDR adds structure and process controls.

**Can I begin investor prep with a free plan?**  
Yes, if your room size and collaboration needs fit free limits.

**What is the easiest decision rule?**  
One-file workflow: secure sharing. Multi-file external process: data room.
