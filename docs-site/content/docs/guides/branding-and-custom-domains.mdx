description: "Verify your own hostname with CNAME and TXT records so share links are served from your domain, then set the company name, website and logo viewers see."

Two things happen on this page, in this order: you prove you own a hostname, and then you decide what viewers see in the header of every shared link.

**Requires:** a configured custom-domain deployment and, on DocKosha Cloud, an
active subscription on a plan that includes the hosted operation. Custom
domains and white-label branding are ordinary application capabilities; see
the [capability matrix](/docs/reference/capability-matrix).

**Who can do this:** workspace owners. Everyone else sees a notice that only owners can manage branding and custom domains.

## Add and verify your domain

1. Go to **Custom domain** in the workspace navigation.
2. Enter the hostname you want to serve links from, for example `docs.example.com`, and click **Add Domain**. The field validates the hostname before the button enables.
3. The domain appears with status **Pending Verification** and DocKosha shows you the DNS records to create:
   - a **CNAME** record whose name is your domain and whose target is the value shown (do not invent it — copy the target from the panel);
   - a **TXT** record at `_cf-custom-hostname.<your domain>` carrying the ownership token;
   - sometimes a second **TXT** record for SSL certificate validation. Some DNS providers display this one with a shortened host label; if it appears in your provider's panel, it is required for the certificate to issue.
4. Create those records with your DNS provider. Each row has a copy button.
5. Click **Verify Records**.

DocKosha re-checks a few times automatically while records propagate, and tells you when it is still waiting. DNS changes can take up to 24–48 hours, so "still pending" is a normal answer, not a failure — come back and click **Verify Records** again.

When both the hostname and its certificate are active, the status becomes **Verified** and your share links are served from your domain.

### If verification does not pass

The panel names the reason:

- _DNS lookup failed_ — the hostname does not resolve yet; confirm it exists.
- _Verification record not found yet_ — the TXT record is missing, or has not propagated.
- _Still pending_ — records are correct but the certificate has not finished issuing. Retry in a few minutes.

## Set what viewers see

The company identity form stays locked until a domain is verified — branding you cannot vouch for is worse than no branding.

1. With the domain verified, scroll to **Company identity** on the same page.
2. Enter your **Company name**.
3. The **Website** field is filled from your verified domain and locked to it.
4. Upload a **logo** — PNG, JPEG, SVG or WebP, up to 2 MB.
5. Generate the **header preview** to see exactly what a viewer will see.
6. Save.

Viewers then see your logo, your company name, a _Verified workspace_ marker and your domain in the viewer header, instead of the default DocKosha framing.

### Removing "Powered by DocKosha"

Whether the viewer header carries the _Powered by DocKosha_ line is decided by the workspace's plan. On a plan that allows white-label branding the line is dropped; otherwise it stays, regardless of your other branding settings.

## Remove a domain

1. Click the trash icon on the domain's row.
2. Confirm.

Removing a **verified** domain disconnects it and **existing links on that domain stop working immediately**. Removing a pending domain just discards the setup. Removal also deprovisions the hostname upstream, so re-adding it starts a fresh verification.

## Limits and operator notes

- A workspace holds **one custom domain at a time**. Remove the current one before adding another; the add form only appears when no domain is configured.
- Custom domains are provisioned through Cloudflare for SaaS. A future authorized operator deployment would need a Cloudflare API token, zone ID and fallback origin configured. External self-hosting is not currently available.
- If the domain configuration cannot be loaded, the panel says so and leaves any existing setup untouched rather than reporting "not verified".
- Watermark branding is configured separately — see [watermarking](/docs/guides/watermarking). Watermarks are burned into the delivered PDF bytes server-side, not drawn over the page in the browser.
- Notification emails come from the operator's configured sender addresses;
  email white-labelling is not part of this guide.
