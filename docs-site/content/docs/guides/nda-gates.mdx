---
title: "Require an NDA before viewing"
description: "Put an NDA in front of a DocKosha link, collect a name and drawn signature, and get a signed PDF delivered to the viewer and the workspace owner."
---

An NDA gate stops a link at a confidentiality agreement. The viewer reads it, types their name, signs it, and only then does the document or data room open. DocKosha generates a signed PDF from what they agreed to and delivers it to both sides.

## How the gate sits in the sequence

Gates are evaluated on the server in this order:

1. Revocation and expiry.
2. Password, if the link has one.
3. Email verification — the viewer enters an address and confirms it with a one-time code.
4. The NDA.

The NDA gate **always requires a verified email address**, because the signature has to be attributable to someone. You do not need to switch the email-verification gate on separately — an NDA-gated link collects and verifies an address on its own.

## Create an NDA template

Templates hold the clauses. You can keep several — a short mutual NDA, a longer one for financial data, whatever your process needs.

1. Open **NDA Templates** from the sidebar. This area is owner-only.
2. Create a template and give it a name you will recognize in the link editor.
3. Edit the body. DocKosha starts you with a general-purpose NDA body covering confidential information, obligations, exclusions, required disclosure, term and return, remedies, and governing law. Replace or amend it to match your own agreement.
4. Preview it. The preview renders the template the way a viewer will see it, with the party, document, and date fields filled in.
5. Save.

The surrounding document — the heading, the disclosing/receiving party block, the covered document name, the effective date, and the signature block — is generated by DocKosha. Your template supplies the clauses in between.

## Put the NDA on a link

1. Create a **new** link for the document or data room.
2. Turn on the NDA requirement.
3. Choose the NDA template to use.
4. Finish configuring the rest of the link and save.

> **The NDA setting is fixed once the link exists.** You cannot add an NDA gate to a link that was created without one, and you cannot switch the gate off on a link that has one. If you need to change it, create a new link and revoke the old one. This is deliberate: it keeps every signature on a link tied to the terms that were actually in force.

## The template is snapshotted onto the link

When you create the link, DocKosha copies the template's body onto that link. From then on, that copy is what viewers sign.

Editing the template afterwards changes what _future_ links snapshot. It does not change any existing link, and it does not change any signature already collected. Archived templates cannot be selected for new links.

## What the viewer does

1. They open the link and clear the password gate, if there is one.
2. They enter their email address and verify it with the one-time code.
3. They read the NDA, which is rendered with their email address and the covered document already filled in.
4. They type their full name and draw a signature.
5. They submit. The document or room opens.

DocKosha generates the signed PDF on the server from the snapshotted terms, the viewer's details, the drawn signature, and the signing timestamp.

A viewer who has already signed on that link with the same verified email address is not asked to sign again on a later visit.

## Where the signed PDF goes

- **The signer** gets a copy by email, as an attachment.
- **The workspace owner** gets a copy by email as well, along with the signer's name and address. (If the owner is the signer, only one copy is sent.)
- **In the app**, a data room's **Analytics** area has a **Signed NDAs** panel listing the viewers who signed on that room's NDA-enabled links, with a download button for each signed PDF.

> **For document links, email is the delivery channel today.** There is no in-app list of signed NDAs on the document analytics page yet — the signed PDF reaches the signer and the workspace owner by email. Keep those emails.

Signature images are accepted as PNG or JPEG up to 1 MB, and are validated before the PDF is generated.

## Honest limits

- **This is not legal advice.** The default template body is a starting point drafted for convenience, not for your jurisdiction, your counterparty, or your deal. Have your own counsel approve the text you actually use.
- **Whether an electronic signature binds depends on your law and your agreement**, not on DocKosha. The gate records what was presented, who verified which email address, what they typed, what they drew, and when.
- **The signature is a drawn image, not a cryptographic digital signature.** DocKosha does not issue certificate-based signatures and makes no claim of conformance to any specific e-signature regime.
- **An NDA gate is a contractual control, not a technical one.** It records agreement before access. It does not stop the person who agreed from doing what they agreed not to. Pair it with [watermarking](/docs/guides/watermarking) for attribution and the [audit log](/docs/guides/audit-log) for evidence.
