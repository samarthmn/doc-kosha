Workspace membership controls what your own team can do **inside DocKosha**. It is a separate system from the gates and access lists that control outside viewers on a public link.

## The access model

There are three moving parts.

**The owner is implicit.** The person who created the workspace is the owner. Ownership is not a role you assign — it is a property of the workspace. The owner always has full access to everything, their access cannot be edited, and they cannot be removed from the workspace.

**Every other member has two access levels, one per surface.**

| Setting                        | Values                                                              | What it covers                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Workspace documents access** | `None`, `Viewer`, `Editor`                                          | Documents outside data rooms — the **My Documents** area.                    |
| **Data rooms access**          | `Custom (select rooms)`, `Viewer (all rooms)`, `Editor (all rooms)` | Data rooms. The "all rooms" values apply to every current _and future_ room. |

**Explicit per-room grants fill the gap.** When data rooms access is set to `Custom (select rooms)`, you pick individual rooms and give the member `Viewer` or `Editor` on each. A member with no workspace-wide room access and no explicit grant for a room cannot open that room at all.

Broadly: `Viewer` can read and copy existing share links; `Editor` can also create and change content and links.

## Invite a teammate

Only the workspace owner can invite.

1. Open **Settings** and stay on the **Profile** tab.
2. Find the **Members** card and choose **Add member**.
3. Enter the person's email address.
4. Set **Workspace documents access**.
5. Set **Data rooms access**. If you choose `Custom (select rooms)`, tick the specific rooms and set `Viewer` or `Editor` for each.
6. Send the invite.

DocKosha emails the invitation. Nothing is granted until the invite is accepted.

### Invite from inside a room

If you are already in a data room, its **Access** area has an **Invite Member** shortcut. It invites the person to the workspace and grants them this room in one step. The shortcut is for people who are not yet workspace members — to grant an existing member a room, change their access instead.

## What the invited person does

1. They open the invite link from the email.
2. They sign in or sign up **with the address the invite was sent to**. Acceptance is matched against that email address; signing in with a different address will not accept the invite.
3. On acceptance, their membership and any room grants are created, and the workspace owner is notified.

Invites expire **14 days** after they are issued. An expired, revoked, or already-accepted invite is rejected when opened.

## Change a member's access

1. **Settings → Profile → Members**.
2. Open the member you want to change.
3. Adjust **Workspace documents access**, **Data rooms access**, or their specific room grants.
4. Save.

The room grants you save replace that member's existing explicit grants — the list you submit is the new complete set, not an addition to it.

If the member has security and workspace emails enabled in their notification preferences, they get an email telling them their access changed.

You can also change a single room's grant from that room's **Access** area, using the **Explicit access** selector next to each member.

## Remove a member

1. **Settings → Profile → Members**.
2. Remove the member and confirm.

Removal deletes their workspace membership and all of their explicit room grants. If they have security and workspace emails enabled, they are notified.

## Revoke a pending invite

Pending invites appear alongside members in the same list, marked **Pending Invite**. Revoke one to stop it from being accepted. A revoked invite cannot be reinstated — issue a new one.

## Role presets

Presets let you save a named combination of documents access, data rooms access, and room grants, then apply it when inviting or editing someone.

Presets are **apply-only**. Applying a preset copies its values onto that member or invite at that moment; it does not link them. Editing the preset later does not change anyone who was created from it.

## Rules that surprise people

- **Only the owner can manage members.** Invites, access changes, and removals are all owner-only.
- **You cannot change your own access**, and you cannot remove yourself from the workspace.
- **The owner cannot be edited or removed.** Owner access is implicit.
- **One workspace per user.** DocKosha currently enforces a single workspace per account. Inviting someone who already belongs to another workspace is rejected.
- **One pending invite per address.** A second invite to an address that already has one pending is rejected — revoke the first one if you want to change its access levels.
- **The Cloud Free plan is a single member.** It allows only the owner, so teammate invites are unavailable until you upgrade. See [Plans and limits](/docs/how-it-works/plans-and-limits).

## What member levels do _not_ control

Member levels have no effect on public links. An outside viewer never becomes a workspace member by opening a link, and a workspace member gets no special treatment when they open a public link — they still face the link's gates.

To scope what an outside viewer sees inside a shared data room, use [room, folder, and document access lists](/docs/guides/access-lists).
